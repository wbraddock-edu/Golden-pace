Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

In plain words: you make a free account with a username and a password or PIN. Your health entries are saved to that account on our server so you can reach them from any device, and a copy is kept on your device so the app works offline. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking tools. Your Google Gemini key never leaves your device. You can delete your account and every record in it at any time.

1. Who we are

Golden Pace is published by William Braddock (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy, please open an issue at our project page or use the contact details on the store listing where you got the app.

2. What Golden Pace saves

When you use Golden Pace, the following is saved in your account on our server and also in your device’s browser storage:

  • Your username and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password or PIN. We cannot see or show back your password or PIN. We also keep a scrambled version of your recovery code.
  • What you enter: blood pressure, pulse, weight, sleep, medicine check-offs, joint comfort, exercise, water, and notes.
  • Meal descriptions, meal photos (shrunk to a smaller size), and the nutrition estimates for them.
  • Balance and chair-stand results, reminder times, and your points, badges and streak.
  • The date you created your account and your last sign-in time.

Saved only on your device and never sent to us: your Google Gemini key, if you type one in (see Section 4), and the app’s own program files that let it open without internet.

3. What we collect and why

Account and health records. We collect what is listed in Section 2 to run your account, keep your records safe, and let you see them on your devices. We use it for nothing else. We do not use your health entries for advertising, profiling, or research, and we do not sell, rent, or share them.

Free guide signup (optional, separate). If you ask for our free ebook on the welcome page, we collect the email address you type in, the date we emailed you, how many times we emailed you, and how many times your guide link was used. We use it only to email you a private download link, and we will ask you separately before sending you anything else. Every email has a “Remove my email address” link that deletes it from our list. This email address is not linked to your Golden Pace account.

Technical details. Our hosting service (Railway) and your web browser handle ordinary technical details such as your internet address and the time of a request. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

4. Meal analysis with Google Gemini (optional)

If you type a Gemini key into Settings and then tap Analyze Meal, your meal description and photo are sent directly from your device to Google using your key, and Google sends back an estimate. Google handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy, and your key belongs to your Google account. We never receive a copy.

Nothing is sent unless you tap the button. If you have not entered a key, nothing is ever sent. Photos can show your surroundings, so please avoid including people, mail, or other private items in a meal photo.

5. Sharing and exporting

We do not sell or share your information. The outside services involved are: our hosting service (Railway), which stores the account data on our behalf; our email service, if you ask for the free guide; and Google, only if you choose meal analysis (Section 4). We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of a person or our service. If you export a spreadsheet (CSV), download a backup file, or print a summary for your doctor, you decide where that file goes. Those files are not protected by your password or PIN, so please keep them somewhere safe.

6. Your password, PIN and recovery code

Your password or PIN is what keeps other people out of your account. It is stored only in scrambled form on our server. After 5 wrong tries the account pauses sign-in for 15 minutes. A PIN is quicker to type but easier to guess than a password, so choose a password if your records matter a lot to you. When you make an account you get a recovery code. It is the only way to reset a forgotten password or PIN. We cannot look it up, and we cannot verify your identity any other way, so if you lose both your password or PIN and your recovery code, your records cannot be opened. On a shared computer, use “Sign out and remove my records from this device”. Please also use your phone’s own screen lock.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and records until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time from My Account in the app menu. Deleting removes your username, records, meal photos and sign-in sessions from our server right away, and from that device. Deleting cannot be undone. Backup files you downloaded stay where you saved them until you delete them. Routine server backups made by our hosting service, if any, are overwritten on their normal schedule. If you asked for our free guide, your email address stays on our list until you remove it with the link in any email we sent you.

8. Children

Golden Pace is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

You can see, correct, export (from the Vault and Reports menus), and delete your records yourself inside the app. Depending on where you live you may have additional privacy rights, such as asking for a copy of, or the deletion of, information we hold. To make a request, open an issue at the address in Section 12 without including any health details, and we will tell you how to verify it is you. For your email address from the free guide, use the removal link in the guide email.

10. Security

Information travels between your device and our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Passwords, PINs and recovery codes are stored only in scrambled form. Sign-in sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies and the server slows down repeated wrong guesses. Your health entries are stored on our hosting service’s private storage. They are not encrypted end-to-end, which means the people who run Golden Pace could technically read them, and we promise not to do so except to fix a problem you ask us to fix, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it. To protect your account, keep your device locked, choose a password or PIN you will remember but others will not guess, keep your recovery code somewhere safe, and keep your device’s software up to date. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, and Golden Pace is not a healthcare provider or a place for emergencies.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date below and, for important changes, tell you in the app. This policy was last updated on September 30, 2026.

12. Contact

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/wbraddock-edu/Golden-pace/issues.
