Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

In plain words: what you write in Golden Pace stays on your own device. We do not have an account system, we do not receive your health entries, and there are no ads or tracking. The one time information leaves your device is when you choose to analyze a meal with Google's Gemini service (Section 4).

1. Who we are

Golden Pace is published by William Braddock (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy, please open an issue at our project page or use the contact details on the store listing where you got the app.

2. What Golden Pace saves on your device

Everything below is saved only in your device’s browser storage, kept separately for each profile:

  • Your profile name, picture, and (if you choose one) a PIN. The PIN is never saved as typed. Only a salted, scrambled version is kept, so the app cannot show it back to you.
  • What you enter: blood pressure, pulse, weight, sleep, medicine check-offs, joint comfort, exercise, water, and notes.
  • Meal descriptions, meal photos (shrunk to a smaller size), and the nutrition estimates for them.
  • Balance and chair-stand results, reminder times, and your points, badges and streak.
  • Your Google Gemini key, only if you type one in (see Section 4).
  • The app’s own program files, saved in your browser so it can open without internet.

3. What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive, see, store, sell, or share the entries you make. Golden Pace has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

The website or app store that delivers Golden Pace to you may record ordinary technical details, such as your internet address and the time, when you download or open the app. That is covered by their own policies, not ours.

4. Meal analysis with Google Gemini (optional)

If you type a Gemini key into Settings and then tap Analyze Meal, your meal description and photo are sent directly from your device to Google using your key, and Google sends back an estimate. Google handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy, and your key belongs to your Google account. We never receive a copy.

Nothing is sent unless you tap the button. If you have not entered a key, nothing is ever sent. Photos can show your surroundings, so please avoid including people, mail, or other private items in a meal photo.

5. Sharing and exporting

We do not share your information. If you export a spreadsheet (CSV), download a backup file, or print a summary for your doctor, you decide where that file goes. Those files are not protected by your PIN, so please keep them somewhere safe.

6. What the PIN does and does not do

A PIN is a privacy screen. It keeps other people who use the same device from opening your profile, and Golden Pace locks itself after 5 minutes away from the screen. It does not scramble your saved records. A person with technical access to the device or browser could still read them. Please also use your phone’s own screen lock.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

Your records stay on your device until you remove them. You can delete a profile and all its records at any time from Profile & Lock in the app menu. Clearing your browser’s site data or uninstalling the app also removes them. Deleting cannot be undone, and we have no copy that could bring records back. Backup files you downloaded stay where you saved them until you delete them.

8. Children

Golden Pace is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

Because your entries live only on your device, you can already see, correct, export, and delete them yourself inside the app. Depending on where you live you may have additional privacy rights. Since we do not hold your information, we cannot look it up or change it for you.

10. Security

Your entries are not sent over the internet by Golden Pace (other than the optional Gemini step). To protect them, keep your device locked, keep your device’s software up to date, choose a PIN you will remember, and save backups in a safe place. No method of storing information is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date below and, for important changes, tell you in the app. This policy was last updated on September 30, 2026.

12. Contact

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/wbraddock-edu/Golden-pace/issues.
