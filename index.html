Movement & Safety Guide

Form tips and gentle rules for home practice

🪑

Chair Tai Chi (Fluidity & Breathing)

Cloud Hands (Seated)

Sit upright away from the backrest. Softly float both hands in front of the chest as if tracing circular clouds in the air. Rotate gently from the torso, not just wrists.

Parting the Wild Horse's Mane

Hold a soft imaginary circle with your hands in front of your chest. Slowly glide one hand forward and outward at shoulder level while pressing the other down. Stay within 70% of maximum stretch.

🚴

Stationary Bike (Low Impact)

1. Seat Height: When pedal is at the lowest position, your knee should have a slight 25°–30° bend, never completely locked out.
2. Talk Test: You must be able to comfortably speak a full sentence out loud while pedaling. If breathless, dial back resistance.
🏋️

Seated Dumbbells (Functional Strength)

Seated Bicep Curl & Scapular Row

Keep elbows pinned gently near ribs for curls. For rows, hinge forward slightly at hips with flat back and pinch shoulder blades together at the top. Perform 10–12 smooth, controlled repetitions.
