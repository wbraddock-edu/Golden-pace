Help & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. Tap a question to open it.

Basics

▶What is Golden Pace?

A simple daily companion for keeping track of your blood pressure, weight, exercise, water, and meals. It also has guided chair tai chi, stationary bike, and light dumbbell routines, plus two short balance and strength checks. Points, levels and badges are there just to make healthy habits feel rewarding.

▶Is it medical advice? Is it a medical device?

No. It is a personal logbook and coach, not a doctor and not a medical device. It cannot diagnose anything. Please talk to your doctor about your readings. For chest pain, trouble breathing, or sudden weakness, call emergency services (911 in the U.S.).

▶Do I need internet?

Not for everyday use. After the first visit, Golden Pace saves itself on your device and opens without a connection. Internet is needed to sign in, for the first load, for saving to your account (this happens by itself when you are back online), and for the optional meal analysis.

▶How do I put it on my phone like a regular app?

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”. Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu, then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”. Installing also helps your phone keep your records safe.

Your account and signing in

▶Do I need an account?

Yes. It is free. You choose a username and either a PIN (4 to 6 numbers) or a password (8 or more characters). Your records are saved to your account, so you can sign in from any phone, tablet or computer and see the same records.

▶Can more than one person use the same phone?

Yes. Each person makes their own account. Use My Account in the menu and choose Sign out, then the next person signs in. On a shared computer, choose “Sign out and remove my records from this device”.

▶Should I choose a PIN or a password?

A PIN is quick to type. A password is harder for someone else to guess. Either one locks after 5 wrong tries for 15 minutes. If your records matter a lot to you, choose a password.

▶What is the recovery code?

When you make your account you get a code like XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Write it on paper and keep it safe. If you forget your password or PIN, tap “I forgot my password or PIN” on the sign-in screen and type your username and this code to choose a new one. You can make a new code any time from My Account.

▶I forgot my password or PIN and lost my recovery code.

We are sorry, but we cannot safely prove who you are without it, so the records in that account cannot be opened. You can make a new account. If you also saved a backup file from the Vault menu, you can restore it into the new account.

▶How do I change my password or PIN?

Open My Account from the menu, then “Change my password or PIN”. You will be signed out on your other devices.

▶How do I delete my account?

Open My Account, then “Delete my account”. This permanently deletes your account and every record in it from our server and this device. It cannot be undone.

Your information and backups

▶Where are my records kept? Is it private?

In your Golden Pace account on our server, and a copy on your device so the app works offline. We do not sell your information or show ads. Your Google Gemini key stays only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

▶What if I lose my phone, or clear my browser data?

Your records are safe in your account. Install Golden Pace on the new phone (or open it in a browser), sign in, and they come back. A backup file from the Persistent Vault menu is still a good extra copy, and Golden Pace reminds you if it has been a while.

▶How do I move to a new phone?

Just sign in with your username and password or PIN. Your records load automatically. Backup files are not protected by your password, so keep them private.

▶How do I share my numbers with my doctor?

Open the Reports tab. You can download a spreadsheet (CSV) or print a one-page summary that includes your username.

▶Can I delete a reading I entered by mistake?

You can correct it: saving again for the same date and time of day (for example, Morning) replaces the earlier entry. Meals can be removed with the ✕ button. There is no button to delete a single reading yet.

Using the app

▶What do the blood pressure colors mean?

They follow common reference ranges: normal (under 120/80), elevated, stage 1, and stage 2. A reading of 180/120 or higher shows an urgent warning: rest for 5 minutes, recheck, and call your doctor, or call emergency services if you have symptoms. The colors are a guide, not a diagnosis.

▶Can I record a morning and an evening reading on the same day?

Yes. Choose the timing (Morning, Evening, and so on) on the entry form. Each timing keeps its own reading for the day.

▶Will the reminders wake my phone?

Not yet. Reminders only sound while Golden Pace is open on the screen. For medicines, please also set an alarm on your phone or clock.

▶What are points, levels, badges and streaks?

A friendly way to notice progress. Each reward can be earned once per day, so ticking things on and off will not add extra points. Your streak grows on days you complete something. Missing a single day is forgiven, and a longer break only lowers it gently. Points have no medical meaning.

▶How do the chair-stand and balance checks work?

For the 30-second chair stand, tap Start and count each full stand. The count only works while the 30 seconds are running. For the balance check, stand right beside a sturdy counter, tap Start, and tap Stop when your foot comes down. Save your result to see it in your history.

▶Something looks wrong or stopped working.

Close and reopen the app first. If you are in a private or incognito window, open it in a normal window, because private windows cannot save records. If the problem continues, please tell us at our project page.

Meal photos and AI

▶How does meal analysis work?

You add your own Google Gemini key in Settings. When you tap Analyze Meal, your description and photo go straight to Google, which sends back a rough estimate of calories, protein, sodium and more. Estimates can be wrong, so always read food labels and follow your doctor’s or dietitian’s advice.

▶Where do I get a Gemini key?

Keys are created in your Google account through Google AI Studio. Google’s terms apply, and any limits or charges on the key are yours. Meal analysis is optional. You can log meals without it.

▶Why does my meal say “Not analyzed”?

The meal was saved, but the AI could not be reached (no key, no internet, or an invalid key). Golden Pace never makes up nutrition numbers. Add or check your key in Settings and log the meal again to get an estimate.
