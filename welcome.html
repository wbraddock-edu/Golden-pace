What is Golden Pace?

A daily logbook and gentle coach. That's it. It is not a doctor, not a medical device, and it will never tell you what to do about your health. It helps you keep track so you and your doctor can decide.

Here's everything you get. Free.

  • ❤️ Blood pressure & weight log

    Morning and evening readings, side by side. Simple color-coded guidance based on standard ranges, plus a clear warning when a reading is in the danger zone.

  • 📈 Charts your doctor can read

    See weeks of readings at a glance. Export everything to a spreadsheet file (CSV) and bring it to your next visit.

  • 🧘 Guided gentle exercise

    Chair tai chi, stationary bike and light dumbbell routines with easy timers. Built for a slower, safer start. Always cleared with your physician first.

  • 🪑 Two quick strength & balance checks

    A 30-second chair-stand test and a balance check you can repeat, so you can watch your own progress over time.

  • 💧 Water, meals & medication reminders

    Tap to count your glasses. Snap a meal photo for an optional nutrition estimate. Set reminders for your routine while the app is open.

  • 🏆 Points, levels & badges

    Small wins add up. Streaks and trophies make healthy habits feel good, and they only reward real activity. No cheating your way up.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Profiles for the whole household

    Two people, one phone, two completely separate sets of records. Add an optional PIN to keep yours private.

  • 📴 Works with no internet

    After your first visit it saves itself on your device. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.

One honest note: if you choose the optional meal-photo analysis, the meal description or photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to get an estimate. Skip that feature and nothing leaves your phone. Read the details in the Privacy Policy.

Who this is for. And who it's not.

✅ It's for you if...

  • You want a simple way to remember and show your numbers
  • You'd like a gentle, low-pressure way to stay active
  • You're tired of apps that are tiny, confusing, or want your email
  • You want to help a parent or spouse keep track

🚫 It's NOT for you if...

  • You want someone to diagnose or treat a condition (see your doctor)
  • You want to change a medication or dose on your own (please don't)
  • You need emergency help (call 911)

Important health & safety notice

  • Consult your primary care physician (or other qualified health provider) before beginning any exercise program, and before changing your diet, medication, or routine.
  • Golden Pace provides general wellness information and a personal logbook. It is not medical advice, does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and is not a medical device.
  • Exercise involves a risk of injury. Start slowly, use a sturdy chair, keep support nearby for balance activities, and stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or chest discomfort.
  • Blood pressure and weight readings in the app are only as accurate as the device you measured with and the numbers you enter. Never ignore or delay medical care because of something you read or did not read here.
  • If you think you may be having a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately.
